# Autonomous Money Agent Roadmap

The end state is an agent-operated revenue loop, but the path cannot skip trust
and payment validation.

## Stage 1: Human-Supervised Revenue Recovery

The agent researches, scores, drafts, configures, monitors, and reports. The user
still sells, asks for payment, and gets account permissions.

Exit:

- At least 2 paid pilots.
- Setup time below 2 hours per merchant.
- Daily report accepted by merchant as useful.

## Stage 2: Semi-Automated Agency

The agent runs prospecting, follow-up, demo setup, pilot monitoring, and renewal
reports. The user handles only calls, payment approval, and exceptions.

Exit:

- Repeatable vertical offer.
- 5 paying merchants.
- Churn risk and support load known.

## Stage 3: Autonomous Operator

The agent can select prospects, run audits, send approved outreach, configure
standard pilots, monitor outcomes, and recommend go/no-go. Human approval remains
required for spend, legal/compliance, and messages sent from real accounts.

Exit:

- Positive unit economics after tool, voice, SMS, and support costs.
- A standard workflow covers 80%+ of installs.
- Owner reports drive renewals without custom analysis.

## Do Not Automate Yet

- Cold outreach from real accounts without human approval.
- Medical or legal advice.
- SMS campaigns without consent and opt-out review.
- Real customer calls before a rollback and human handoff path exists.
