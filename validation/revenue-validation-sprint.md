# 7 Day Revenue Validation Sprint

Goal: prove that a real buyer will pay for missed-call recovery before building
a larger AI receptionist platform.

## Offer

Sell this, not generic AI:

```text
We recover missed calls within 60 seconds, capture the job details, notify the
owner, and report how many leads were recovered.
```

Default paid pilot:

- Pilot: AUD 300 for 7 days
- Monthly: AUD 499 if useful leads are recovered
- Scope: missed-call textback or after-hours intake only
- Kill rule: no free pilots unless the merchant provides real missed-call data

## Agent Work

The agent can do:

- Build a 100 prospect list.
- Score prospects with `make score-prospects`.
- Draft personalized audits and outreach.
- Prepare ROI math and proposal text.
- Configure runtime, smoke tests, and dry-run demo.
- Track pilot metrics and generate daily reports.

## Human Work

The user must do:

- Pick one vertical.
- Call or message business owners.
- Ask for money or a paid pilot deposit.
- Get permission to use real phone and message flows.
- Confirm legal/compliance risk for the selected vertical.
- Decide go/no-go based on payment evidence.

## Discovery Questions

Ask about past behavior, not future interest:

1. How many calls did you miss last week?
2. How quickly do you call back missed calls today?
3. What is one new customer worth?
4. What happens after hours?
5. Who owns follow-up when staff are busy?
6. Would you pay today for a 7 day pilot if we can start with missed-call recovery?

## Go / No-Go

Go when one of these is true:

- 2 paid pilots in 7 days.
- 1 paid pilot plus 2 scheduled installs.
- A merchant gives real call data and pays a deposit.

Pivot when:

- 5+ serious conversations but no payment.
- Buyers like the concept but reject the offer or pricing.

No-go when:

- 100 targeted touches produce fewer than 5 qualified conversations.
- 15 discovery calls produce zero payment or zero call-data access.
- Every install needs more than 2 hours of custom work.
